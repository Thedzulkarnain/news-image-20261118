This entry is a single-page newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026, headlined with the date and the line "45 STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ALL FROM WEDNESDAY".

Across the top sits a stats strip, followed by three illustrated lead stories: air defences in Kuwait and Bahrain intercepting Iranian drones, Xi greeting Trump in Shenzhen, and Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth. Below them are ten more stories, then a world map marked with numbered pins, and an "ALSO ON WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER" list carrying 32 further stories, numbered 14 to 45. A DATE CHECK box notes stories that were deliberately left out because they were first reported on 17 November, so everything on the page belongs to the 18th.

The image is `art.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`. It runs with Node 22 and needs no network access.
